- **[m7-mass-closure](https://github.com/SergioWatanabe/m7-mass-closure):** a reproduction of the M7 aerosol emulator from Harder et al. (2022). Projecting the network's output onto the mass constraint makes conservation exact (relative residual about 1e-13) with no measurable accuracy loss. The completion closure instead degrades the `BC_cs` channel on every seed, because that channel is what's left after much larger sibling modes nearly cancel (about 2400 times smaller than their summed magnitudes).
